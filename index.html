About Me

TheMattEmpire is a Solo Game Developer that is taking everything he has learned over 25 years of Design and Education to create unique digital experiences including VR/XR games as well as Overlay Experiences for the Desktop.

Matt has experience in Game development, Installation design, Toy concepting, 3D Previsualization, Web design, and Graphic Design. In regards to 3D assets he has the full skillset including 3D Modelling, Sculpting, Retopology, UV Unwrapping, Texturing, (basic) Rigging, and Animation.

At the moment he is working on an Asteroids-Like experience in VR/XR as well as a productivity app that is an Overlay of your desktop offering a task list that can feed a pet dragon.
